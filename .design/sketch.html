Tidy Pack – sketch

Roughly how the game's bag window looks with Tidy Pack, at the default scale. The window, the search box and the money are the game's own – Tidy Pack only orders the items in groups. Fonts and images differ a little in the game. Hover a slot to see what item it is.

Bag buttons and menus
The game's merchant window
(with the game's own button "Sell All Junk")
  • The window is the game's own bag window (all bags in one). The game decides where it is: at the bottom right, above the bag buttons. It is 12 slots wide (514 units) and the height follows the content. The bag icon at the top left no longer opens the game's bag menu.
  • Eleven groups in a fixed order, without parts and lines: Favorites, New, Consumables, Equipment, Trade Goods, Quest Items, Keys, Bags, Miscellaneous, Junk and Empty. Empty groups don't show. Within a group similar items sit side by side (potions before food, weapons before armor, recipes last in Trade Goods), then by quality, name and stack size. Small groups share a line.
  • New gathers what you got since you last opened the bags (here Emberglow Ring and Gnoll Paw). They stay there until you close the window.
  • Starred (here Hearthstone) goes to Favorites at the top. With the mouse: Alt-click the item. With a controller: pick up the item and put it on ★ at the top right. ★ is faint until you hold an item – tick "Holding an item". The stars apply to all your characters.
  • Locks: quest items, normal items a quest needs (here Linen Cloth) and items in your equipment sets. The lock shows only at a merchant, and then the items can't be clicked. The tooltip says why, with a red line.
  • Sell Junk shows above the bag window while you trade and there is junk. It sells grey items except locked and starred ones, at most 12 per click. The heading Junk shows what the junk is worth.
  • On the slots: level and BoE only on uncommon or better weapons and armor, and the count at the bottom right. Grey items get the game's junk coin at a merchant.
  • Free slots show as a slot with the count. The number turns red at 5 or fewer, and when the bags get completely full, "Your bags are full." shows in the middle of the screen.
  • Bags: those you wear don't show among the items. Green arrow in Bags = bigger than your smallest bag; right-click (or Equip with the controller) to swap.
  • Introduction (proposal): short tips in the game's own tip box, one at a time and never in combat. Each tip shows once (saved for the whole account). With the mouse the box closes with the button. With a controller the box has no button and disappears when the bags close, and the text shows the buttons of your controller. Pick a tip under "Introduction" above.
  • Full bags: when looting or skinning fails because the bags are full, a question shows right under the loot window, in the same kind of panel and as wide: which item can be deleted so the new one fits. One press deletes. Pick "Full bags while skinning" above.
  • Quests ahead: with Questie installed, items a quest needs later get the map's yellow exclamation mark at the top right and a line in the tooltip. Pick "Quests ahead" above.
  • Junk: Alt-right-click or the junk coin next to ★ marks an item as junk. It gets the game's own junk coin at the top left. Pick "Marked as junk" above.
  • Controller: the cursor starts on what you just got (otherwise at the top), LB and RB jump to the previous or next group, and the D-pad goes to the nearest slot in the direction you press. At a merchant A and X do nothing on locked items.

Money on your characters – proposal

A sketch, not the game. Point at the money at the bottom right of the bag window, and a tooltip shows how much each of your characters on this realm has, and the total – the same kind of tooltip as "N free". Each character saves its money when you play it (together with its item counts), so a character shows up after you have logged in with it once.

2. When there is no tooltip, and how to turn it off

Only one character (and nothing in the account bank): no tooltip – the money row already says it all.
Clicking the money works as before: the game's own box for picking up money opens (for trading or mail).
Only characters on this realm, the same as the item counts. Gear → Bag and bank → Forget a Character removes their money too.

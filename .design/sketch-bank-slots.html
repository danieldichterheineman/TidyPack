Bank slots and bank bags – proposal

A sketch, not the game. In WoW Forever the bank is the main bank (28 slots) plus up to 7 bag slots that you buy one at a time and fill with bags – like your own bags. Today the Bank tab shows "? Tab 1" and nothing about the bag slots. The proposal: the bottom row of the Bank tab shows the bank's own slots, as the Bags tab shows your bags.

Counts on your other characters – proposal

A sketch, not the game. Every character you play saves how many of each item it has in its bags (the reagent bag too) and, at a banker, in its bank. When you point at an item, the tooltip lists the characters on this realm that have it. Not counted: mail, what characters wear, the auction house and the account bank (not in Tidy Pack yet). A character shows up after you have logged in with it once, and its bank after a visit to a banker.

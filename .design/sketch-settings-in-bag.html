Tidy Pack – sketch: counts in the tooltip, the gear menu and options in the bags

A sketch, not the game. Three proposals. 1: a colleague's idea – the tooltip shows how many you have in the bags and in the bank, and the total. 2: the gear menu gathers all "Show …" options in a submenu. 3: the junk list and the groups open in the bag window itself, like the icon choice in Sets, so Tidy Pack needs no page of its own in the game's options.

1a. A colleague's proposal: counts in the bags and the bank in the tooltip

Shown only when the item is in the bank – otherwise the lines say nothing new. The bags are counted by the game (C_Item.GetItemCount). The bank is counted from Tidy Pack's own saved bank, so the count shows far from the banker too.

At a banker it just says "Bank". Further away it says when the bank was saved, the same words the Bank tab uses. Only this character: other characters' bags were rejected (AGENTS.md).

1c. Shorter variant, one line (chosen)

Bags 12 · Bank 40 · Total 52
Takes less room in a tooltip that already has lines from Tidy Pack (set, group, quest). 1a is easier to read on a TV; 1c takes one line instead of three. I propose 1c.

How to use it
• Press B: your items are sorted into groups.
• Alt-click an item: favorite, junk list, set or group.
• The gear button ⚙ on the bag window has every option.
• Tabs: Bank (at a banker), Sets and Quests.

No options here: everything is under the gear. Name and version are read from the TOC file (## Author, ## Version). The link stands in a box where it is already selected, because an addon can't open the browser; on Windows it says Ctrl+C. The address can also be put as ## X-Website in the TOC file.

Things to consider

Long listsScroll with the mouse wheel, like the Quests tab. The window keeps its size.

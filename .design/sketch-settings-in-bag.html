3c. Spelets inställningar: om Tidy Pack, länk och kort hjälp (vald)

Options – AddOns – Tidy Pack
Tidy Pack 1.2.0
By Daniel Dichter Heineman
How to use it
• Press B: your items are sorted into groups.
• Alt-click an item: favorite, junk list, set or group.
• The gear button ⚙ on the bag window has every option.
• Tabs: Bank (at a banker), Sets and Quests.

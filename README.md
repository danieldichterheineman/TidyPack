# Tidy Pack

Tidy Pack sorts the game's own bag window into clear groups, so you find what you need at a
glance – made for WoW Forever, with mouse or controller.

Tidy Pack doesn't replace your bags with a new window. It keeps the game's own backpack,
search box and money display, and simply puts your items in order. Everything the game lets
you do with an item – use, equip, sell, split, link – works as usual, also with a controller.

## Features

- **Groups**: Favorites, New, Consumables, Equipment, Trade Goods, Quest Items, Keys, Spare
  Bags, Miscellaneous and Junk. Empty groups are hidden, and similar items sit side by side.
  The "Add to bag" slot next to Favorites shows how many slots are free – drop an item on it
  to put it in your bags.
- **Your own groups**: make groups like "Potions" or "Food & Drink" – Alt-click an item to put it
  in one, or add a whole type at once ("Add all Potion items to Group"). Manage them
  under Esc → Options → AddOns → Tidy Pack → Groups.
- **Quests**: quest items show how many you have and need ("3/7") and which quest wants them. The
  Quests tab lists your quests with ready ones first – open a quest to see its items and what is
  still missing. Extra stacks of an item that a quest only needs some of can be sold.
- **Special bags** – quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags, profession bags and the reagent bag –
  get their own groups at the bottom, with the bag's icon, name and free slots.
- **New loot** is shown at the top until you close your bags.
- **Favorites**: keep items like your Hearthstone at the top.
- **Sets**: collect items under a name and icon you pick, like "Fishing", in the Sets tab.
  Click a set to put it all on. Missing items are greyed out, and a dot on the tab tells you
  something is missing.
- **Locked items**: quest items, items your active quests ask for and items in your sets
  can't be sold by mistake at a merchant. Their tooltip tells you why. You can turn the locks
  off in the settings.
- **Junk list**: whenever you loot an item on the list, Tidy Pack asks whether to delete it.
- **Bags full**: when something can't be looted, Tidy Pack suggests an item to delete.
  Nothing is ever deleted until you press Delete.
- **Item level and "BoE"** on uncommon or better gear, and a free-slot count that turns red
  when you run low.
- **Quick bag swaps**: right-click a bigger bag to replace your smallest one.
- **Questie support** (optional): items that quests you haven't taken yet will need get a
  yellow exclamation mark and are never suggested for deletion.
- **Controller**: the cursor starts on your newest loot, LB and RB jump between groups, and
  the D-pad moves to the nearest item.

## How to use

- **Item options**: hold Alt to see a small "..." on every item, then Alt-click one for a menu:
  favorite, junk list, sets and your own groups. With a controller, pick the item up and drop
  it on the gear button.
- **Gear button** (next to the close button): click it for your junk list, groups and settings.
- **Sets tab**: click "New Set", then use the set's "..." button and choose "Add What I'm
  Wearing", or drop items on the set. The "..." button also renames the set, changes its
  icon, removes an item or deletes it.
- **Quests tab**: click a heading to open it, then a quest to see its items.
- **Settings**: type `/tidypack`, use the minimap's addon menu, or go to Esc → Options →
  AddOns → Tidy Pack.
- **Key bindings**: Esc → Options → Keybindings → AddOns → Tidy Pack, to open the Sets tab
  or equip sets 1–5.

## Known limitations

- Sets can't be put on in combat – the game doesn't allow anyone to change gear then.
- Locks and the controller cursor can't change during combat. They
  catch up as soon as combat ends.
- Quest locks match item names in English, the game's language.
- The merchant's own "Sell All Junk" button sells every grey item. It doesn't know about
  Tidy Pack's locks or your junk list.
- Quest hints need Questie, whose data for Forever's new quests isn't complete yet.
- Tidy Pack turns on the game's "Combined Bags" once, at your first login.

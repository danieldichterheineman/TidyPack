# Tidy Pack

Tidy Pack sorts the game's own bag window into clear groups, so you find what you need at a
glance – made for WoW Forever, with mouse or controller.

Tidy Pack doesn't replace your bags with a new window. It keeps the game's own backpack and
money display, and simply puts your items in order. Everything the game lets
you do with an item – use, equip, sell, split, link – works as usual, also with a controller.

## Features

- **Groups**: Favorites, New, Consumables, Equipment, crafting materials by type, Recipes & Gems,
  Quest Items, Keys, Miscellaneous, Spare Bags and Junk. Empty groups are hidden, and similar items
  sit side by side.
  The groups come in five parts: your own, everyday, crafting, quests and the rest. Your free
  slots are shown at the bottom left.
- **Your own groups**: make groups like "Potions" or "Food & Drink" – Alt-click an item to put it
  in one, or add a whole type at once ("Add All "Potion" Items to Group"). Manage them from the
  gear button, right in the bag window.
- **Quests**: quest items show how many you have and need ("3/7") and which quest wants them. The
  Quests tab lists your quests with ready ones first – open a quest to see its items and what is
  still missing. Extra stacks of an item that a quest only needs some of can be sold.
- **Bank tab**: at a banker, your bank is sorted in the same groups as your bags. Right-click or
  drag items between your bank and your bags, or drop an item on an empty spot to put it in
  your bank. Away from the bank, the tab shows what your bank held the last time you were there.
- **Bag and bank counts**: item tooltips show how many you have in your bags and in your bank
  ("Bags 12 · Bank 40 · Total 52").
- **Crafting materials** are grouped by type (Cloth, Herb, Leather …), with the ones your
  professions use first.
- **Your bags at a glance**: the Tidy Pack logo replaces the game's bag buttons, and your bags
  are shown as small icons at the bottom of the bag window – drop a bigger bag on one to swap it.
- **Drop anywhere**: hold an item over your bags and drop it on any empty spot.
- **Special bags** – quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags, profession bags and the reagent bag –
  get their own part at the bottom, with the bag's icon, name and free slots.
- **Show by bag** (optional): each bag and bank tab on its own rows, in slot order with its empty
  slots, with Favorites and your own groups still at the top.
- **Search box** (optional): search in the title bar of the Bags and Bank tabs – matches glow
  blue and the rest dims.
- **New loot** is shown at the top until you close your bags.
- **Favorites**: keep items like your Hearthstone at the top.
- **Locked items**: quest items, items your active quests ask for and items in the game's
  equipment sets can't be sold by mistake at a merchant. Their tooltip tells you why. You can
  turn the locks off in the gear button's menu.
- **Junk list**: whenever you loot an item on the list, Tidy Pack asks whether to delete it.
- **Bags full**: when something can't be looted, Tidy Pack suggests an item to delete.
  Nothing is ever deleted until you press Delete.
- **Upgrade arrows** on gear you can use that is better than what you wear.
- **Item level and "BoE"** on uncommon or better gear, and a free-slot count that turns red
  when you run low.
- **Quick bag swaps**: right-click a bigger bag to replace your smallest one.
- **Questie support** (optional): items that quests you haven't taken yet will need get a
  yellow exclamation mark and are never suggested for deletion.
- **Controller**: the cursor starts on your newest loot, LB and RB jump between groups, and
  the D-pad moves to the nearest item.
- **Your language**: Tidy Pack follows the game's language – English, Deutsch, Español,
  Français, Italiano, Português, Русский, 한국어, 简体中文 or 繁體中文 – or the one you pick under
  the gear button → Language.

## How to use

- **Item options**: hold Alt (Option on a Mac) to see a small "..." on every item, then
  Alt-click one for a menu: favorite, junk list and your own groups. With a controller, pick the
  item up and drop it on the gear button.
- **Gear button** (next to the close button): all options, in three menus (Show, Merchants and
  loot, Bag and bank), plus your junk list and your own groups, which open in the bag window.
- **Quests tab**: click a heading to open it, then a quest to see its items.
- **Language**: gear button → Language. "Automatic" follows the game's language. Press "Reload
  Now" (or type `/reload`) to switch.
- **Settings**: right-click the Tidy Pack logo, or use `/tidypack` or the minimap's addon menu
  with your bags open, for the gear button's menu. With your bags closed, `/tidypack` opens
  Esc → Options → AddOns → Tidy Pack, which has a short guide and a link to updates.

## Known limitations

- Locks and the controller cursor can't change during combat. They catch up as soon as combat
  ends.
- Quest locks match item names in the game's language.
- The merchant's own "Sell All Junk" button sells every grey item. It doesn't know about
  Tidy Pack's locks or your junk list.
- Quest hints need Questie, whose data for Forever's new quests isn't complete yet.
- Tidy Pack turns on the game's "Combined Bags" once, at your first login.
- The reagent bag's items stay in their own part at the bottom; they aren't mixed into the other
  groups.
- The Bank tab shows your character's bank, not the account-wide bank. With a controller, the
  game's own bank window is used.
- The translations are machine-made. Corrections are very welcome – leave a comment or open an
  issue. Item names, item types and the game's own buttons stay in the game's language.

# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Favorites: Alt-click an item, or pick it up and drop it on the star button, to keep it at
  the top of your bags. Do the same again to remove it. Favorites are shared by all your
  characters.
- New: items you have looted since you last opened your bags are shown at the top until you
  close them.
- Locked items: quest items, ordinary items that one of your active quests asks for, and
  items in your equipment sets can't be clicked while you talk to a merchant, so you can't
  sell them by mistake. Their tooltip shows which quest needs them.
- Sell Junk: at a merchant, a button above your bags sells your grey items – never locked or
  starred ones – 12 at a time, so everything can be bought back. (With a controller, use the
  merchant's own "Sell All Junk" button.)
- The Junk group shows what your junk is worth.
- The free-slot count turns red when 5 or fewer slots are left, and you get a warning when
  your bags become full.
- Cleaner window: 11 groups instead of 15, item level and "BoE" only on uncommon or better
  gear, locks only at merchants, and no bag menu or clean-up button.

## 0.1.0 (not released)

- The game's bag window shows everything you carry, sorted into fixed groups.
- Item level and "BoE" on gear, one slot that counts your free space, and quick bag swaps:
  right-click a bigger bag to replace your smallest one.
- Works with mouse and controller.
